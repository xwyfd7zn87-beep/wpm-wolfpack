▸ Technical Overview

Platform Capabilities

WPM Aeromed operates a mixed fleet of fixed-wing and multi-rotor UAV systems, configured for specific mission profiles.

  • Payload DeliveryMedical supplies, AEDs, blood products, naloxone kits
  • Thermal ImagingFLIR sensors for night ops, SAR, and heat-signature mapping
  • Live Video Relay4K HD and thermal feeds streamed to ground command in real time
  • BVLOS OperationsBeyond-visual-line-of-sight capability (FAA waiver coordination)
  • Night OperationsLow-light navigation lighting and strobe systems for nocturnal missions
  • Multi-Aircraft CoordinationSwarm-capable operations for wide-area coverage
Max PayloadUp to 5 kg (mission-dependent)
Operational RangeUp to 15 km (standard config)
Flight Time25–45 min (varies by platform)
Cruise Speed45–80 km/h

▸ Use Cases

Real-World Applications

WPM Aeromed is built for the scenarios where speed and reliability are non-negotiable.

Rural Medical Delivery

Bridge coverage gaps in underserved areas — delivering critical medications and supplies faster than any ground vehicle can.

Cardiac Emergency Response

AED delivery ahead of EMS arrival. Every second counts in cardiac arrest — UAVs can beat response times by minutes.

Disaster Relief Logistics

First-in aerial delivery when roads are blocked. Post-flood, post-storm, or ildfire situations where ground access is impossible.

Wildfire & Hazmat Overwatch

Thermal overwatch for firefighters and hazmat teams — real-time fire mapping and hotspot detection without putting crews at risk.

Law Enforcement Support

Aerial overwatch, suspect tracking, crowd monitoring, and rapid evidence documentation with encrypted video feeds.

Hospital & Clinic Resupply

Scheduled UAV resupply routes between facilities — blood, organs, lab samples, and emergency pharmaceuticals on demand.

Built on a Veteran Standard

WPM Aeromed was conceived by veterans who understand what it means to operate under pressure in austere environments. Every system we deploy is built to that standard — redundant, reliable, and ready.
